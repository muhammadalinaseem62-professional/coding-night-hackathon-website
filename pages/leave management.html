Leave Policy Notes

Annual leave: Planned time off requested in advance.
Sick leave: Absence due to illness or health needs.
Casual leave: Short-term leave for personal matters.
Unpaid leave: Leave that is not paid by the employer.

These are general categories. Configure the actual entitlement, eligibility and approval rules according to your organization's policy.
